About AHADIX

Digital solutions agency founded by Abdul Ahad

AHADIX is a digital solutions agency that builds complete online products for businesses — websites, web applications, eCommerce platforms, SEO programs and workflow automation. Founded by Abdul Ahad and based in Lahore, Pakistan, we work with companies and startups worldwide.

From design to deployment, we deliver modern frontends, reliable backends, APIs, payment integrations, admin dashboards and online stores. We also build automation flows and AI-ready systems that help teams work smarter.

Every project prioritizes clean architecture, performance and measurable growth — with SEO, automation and ongoing support available after launch.
